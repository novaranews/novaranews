# Code of Conduct

NovaNews contributors and community members are expected to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

When reporting a conduct issue, use a private maintainer contact or GitHub's reporting tools rather than a public issue. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
